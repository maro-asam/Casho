# Social Formats and Production Checklist

## Canvas defaults

| Asset | Canvas | Aspect |
|---|---:|---:|
| Feed post | 1080 × 1350 px | 4:5 |
| Carousel slide | 1080 × 1350 px | 4:5 |
| Story | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 |
| Reel scene | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 |
| Reel cover | 1080 × 1920 px | 9:16 |

These are working production defaults. Check current platform requirements before a time-sensitive campaign export.

## Mobile-first review
- Preview at actual phone scale.
- Check headline readability without zooming.
- Check Arabic ligatures, shaping, punctuation, and RTL flow.
- Check that no important content sits beneath Instagram interface overlays.
- Avoid thin type on busy imagery.
- Check contrast in both bright and dark viewing environments.

## Carousel review
- Hook is clear on slide 1.
- Each slide advances the story.
- No repeated filler slides.
- Consistent grid, margins, typography, and visual language.
- Visual rhythm changes where needed.
- Final slide has a clear conclusion or CTA.

## Reels review
- Hook lands in the first 1–2 seconds.
- Every scene has a purpose.
- Timing allows the viewer to read.
- Motion is smooth and intentional.
- Visual continuity is maintained.
- End card is legible and on-brand.
- Cover remains readable in profile-grid crop.

## Delivery package
Where possible, deliver:
- Editable project/design.
- PNG/JPG exports for static assets.
- MP4 for finished motion only when the tool actually supports video export.
- Individual carousel slides plus a preview of the sequence.
- A text/copy sheet.
- A short note listing fonts, assets, and any substitutions.
