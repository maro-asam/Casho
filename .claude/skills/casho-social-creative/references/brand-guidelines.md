# Casho Brand Guidelines — Working Social Edition

This is a working guide based on the currently established Casho direction. It is not a substitute for an official brand book.

## Brand
- Name: Casho / كاشو
- Product context: connects POS with an online store so business operations are more connected and managed in one place.
- Brand character: modern, clear, useful, confident, approachable, ambitious.

## Core visual identity
- Primary color: sample from the official Casho logo supplied by the user. Do not guess or hard-code an unverified hex value.
- Core supporting colors: white, near-black, and neutral grays.
- Arabic typeface: Cairo.
- Preferred design direction: Apple-inspired premium minimalism.
- Social design should be recognizable as Casho even when the logo is not the largest element.

## Logo handling
- Use the official logo file supplied by the user.
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space.
- Do not redraw, distort, rotate, crop, recolor, outline, or apply effects.
- Avoid placing the logo on backgrounds where contrast is insufficient.
- If a transparent/light/dark logo variant is needed but unavailable, ask for the correct asset.

## Copy voice
- Default: natural Egyptian Arabic.
- Tone: confident, clear, smart, human, and direct.
- Explain business value in simple language.
- Avoid exaggerated claims, corporate jargon, and forced slang.
- Keep headlines concise and memorable.

## Current campaign-stage caution
Casho has previously been described as pre-announcement / teaser-stage. This may have changed. Always follow the latest instruction in the current brief about whether the brand or product may be revealed. Never assume teaser status is permanent.

## Asset checklist
Request or inspect:
- Official logo (preferably SVG or transparent PNG).
- Exact primary blue or official brand guide, if available.
- Product screenshots or screen recordings only when the content needs them.
- Approved product facts and feature list.
- Existing social posts the user considers on-brand.
