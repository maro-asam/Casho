# Apple-Inspired Art Direction

Use Apple as a quality and design-philosophy reference, not as a template to copy.

## What to borrow
- Extreme clarity: one idea per composition.
- Confident scale: large, deliberate typography.
- Negative space: let the subject breathe.
- Product-first storytelling: show the meaningful thing, not decoration.
- Precise alignment and optical balance.
- Restrained color and controlled contrast.
- Minimal copy with strong editorial pacing.
- Motion that communicates a transformation or reveals information.
- High craft in small details: kerning, spacing, crop, shadow, and transitions.

## What not to copy
- Apple's exact campaign layouts, slogans, product photography, iconography, packaging, or recognizable compositions.
- Apple logos or product imagery presented as Casho assets.
- A simplistic white background plus oversized headline on every single post.

## Composition patterns to explore
- Oversized Arabic headline with a small, precise supporting line.
- One central object or metaphor with generous negative space.
- Split composition contrasting chaos and clarity.
- Cropped macro detail to create intrigue.
- Strong typographic poster with one brand-color accent.
- Sequential reveal across carousel slides.
- Before/after transformation with a clear visual cause.
- Minimal dark frame used selectively for dramatic storytelling.

## Motion principles
- Animate with intention: reveal, align, connect, simplify, resolve.
- Prefer smooth, controlled easing and purposeful timing.
- Use transitions that reinforce the message rather than distract.
- Avoid excessive bounce, random particles, constant zooming, and template-like kinetic typography.
- Use sound design to support the story, not overwhelm it.
